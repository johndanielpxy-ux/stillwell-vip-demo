## Design Context

### Users
People living with chronic conditions who want to organise reports, record day-to-day symptoms and prepare questions for appointments. The current release is a fictional-profile prototype for a five-minute VIP pitch, viewed mainly over a shared desktop screen.

### Brand Personality
Calm, personal, clear. The user's Juno/Guava references inform the conversational capture and organised health-history workflows. These are established session preferences, not inferred from the code.

### Aesthetic Direction
User-approved palette from the final pitch deck: white #FFFFFF primary surfaces, cherry blossom pink #F6D5E2 secondary accents, deep plum #4D2945 text and actions. The user rejected alternating page backgrounds and unnecessary colours. Keep the existing approved typography and layout during this colour-only revision. Exact approved colours take precedence over generic skill advice against pure white.

### Design Principles
- One white canvas across every route; pink highlights controls and small components.
- Plum carries legible text, active controls and focus indicators.
- Preserve the rehearsed record → journal → appointment-brief flow.
- Charts and status values remain distinguishable by labels/shapes as well as colour.
- Judge actual desktop and mobile renders, not only token values.
